About

The person behind the lens

I'm Harsh Barve. This site began as a simple habit — walking out with the camera, paying a little more attention to the light, and keeping the frames that stayed with me. Through Harsh's Lens is that habit, made public.

Almost everything here is made with a Nikon D3500. It isn't the newest body on the shelf, but it taught me that the camera matters far less than the looking. Most of these photographs were made slowly, in available light, without much interference afterwards.

The collection is organised loosely — streets, nature, architecture, people and the odd experiment — but the thread is the same: honest moments, kept honestly. New frames are added as they earn their place.
